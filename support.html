How Can We Help?

Quick answers to the most common questions.

⏳ Why hasn't my offer been credited yet?

Most offers credit automatically within a few minutes, but some partner networks take up to 24–72 hours to confirm completion. Please make sure you completed every step of the offer exactly as instructed.

💸 How long does a withdrawal take?

Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed by our team for security. Most requests are processed within a few business days. You'll see the status update in your Wallet history.

🚫 My account was banned — why?

Accounts are suspended for violating our Terms of Service — such as creating multiple accounts, using bots/VPN to fake location, or submitting fraudulent offer/task completions. If you believe this was a mistake, contact us with your account email.

📋 My social task was rejected — what now?

Social tasks are reviewed manually. If proof wasn't clear or the task wasn't completed as instructed, it may be rejected. You're welcome to reattempt the task and resubmit correct proof.

👥 My referral isn't showing up

Referral bonuses are credited once your invited friend verifies their email and becomes active on the platform. Make sure they signed up using your exact referral link or code.

🔑 I forgot my password

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen — a reset link will be sent to your registered email.
