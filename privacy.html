Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's what we collect and why.

1️⃣ Information We Collect

Account information: name, email address, password (encrypted), and country, provided when you register.

Usage data: offers completed, ads watched, tasks submitted, referral activity, and earning/withdrawal history.

Payment details: the payout account information you provide (such as bKash/Nagad number, PayPal email, or crypto wallet address) — used solely to process your withdrawals.

Device & technical data: device type, operating system, app version, and a push-notification token, collected automatically to keep the App working correctly and to deliver notifications.

2️⃣ How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To credit earnings and process withdrawal requests
  • To send important notifications (balance updates, withdrawal status, offers)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, multiple accounts, and abuse
  • To improve the App's features and performance

3️⃣ Third-Party Services We Use

EARNOVA relies on the following trusted third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Supabase — securely stores your account and app data
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) — delivers push notifications to your device
  • Google AdMob — displays ads within the App and may collect an advertising ID for ad personalization and measurement
  • Offer/CPA partner networks — process offer completions you choose to participate in, and may receive limited data (such as your user ID and device/country info) to verify completion

4️⃣ Advertising & Analytics

We use Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads and measure performance. You can manage ad personalization through your device's system settings (e.g. Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).

5️⃣ Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections and access controls. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6️⃣ Data Retention

We retain your account and earning history for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations. You may request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 8).

7️⃣ Children's Privacy

EARNOVA is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will take steps to remove that account and associated data.

8️⃣ Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Contact page.

9️⃣ Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated in the App. Continued use of EARNOVA after an update means you accept the revised policy.

📩 Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out via our Contact page.
